What kind of space is this?

To describe a life, a line is not enough. It can show before and after, but not depth — not the way one moment can echo for decades, or one person can widen the whole world.

A life is not just chronological. It is dimensional: it has meaning, relation, aspiration, and time — inward, outward, upward, and forward.

LifeOS doesn't reduce you to a profile or a score. It holds the shape of what you've chosen to share, only as long as you want it held, and never claims to know what you haven't said.

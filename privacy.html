Privacy Policy

Effective: June 28, 2026 · Airlock Technologies LLC · Contact: admin@airlocklabs.io

The short version: Airlock is built local-first and sovereignty-first. We collect the minimum to run the Service. We do not sell your data. We do not train shared models on your personal data — Otto recalls your information through retrieval, not by baking it into model weights, which is exactly why your data stays deletable. Ask us to delete it and it's gone.

1. What we collect

  • Account / identity: a Discord user ID (and, if you verify, a linked-role credential) so we can recognize you and gate membership.
  • What you send Otto: the prompts and content you choose to share when you invoke the app, plus any notes or memories you save.
  • Basic operational logs: timestamps and error/diagnostic data needed to keep the Service running.

2. What we do with it

Only to provide the Service to you: to respond to your prompts, recall what you've shared, and operate and secure the system. That's it.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not train shared/foundation models on your personal data.
  • We do not build profiles of you for advertising, and we do not collate your activity across platforms.

4. Retention and deletion

Your data is stored only as long as needed to provide the Service or as you keep it. Because we use retrieval rather than fine-tuning, deletion is real: you can request removal of your data at any time by emailing admin@airlocklabs.io, and we will delete it from our stores.

5. Third parties

To operate, the Service passes data to providers such as Discord (the surface you interact on) and AI model providers (to generate responses). Those providers process data under their own privacy policies. We share only what is necessary for the feature you are using.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage of secrets and credentials. No system is perfectly secure, but minimizing what we hold is our first line of defense.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR/CCPA), you may have additional rights. Contact admin@airlocklabs.io.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes are reflected by the "Effective" date above.

What Meta shipped

On March 25, 2026, eight researchers at Meta's Fundamental AI Research lab (FAIR) in Paris — working alongside École Normale Supérieure (ENS-PSL) — published a paper titled "A foundation model of vision, audition, and language for in-silico neuroscience." They called the model TRIBE v2. The acronym stands for TRImodal Brain Encoder.

The model does one thing: given any video, audio, or text stimulus, it predicts which regions of a human brain will activate in response. Not a self-report. Not a behavioral survey. The actual fMRI signal.

They trained it on 1,000+ hours of fMRI recordings across 720 human subjects, spanning eight datasets (4 training, 4 testing). The stack underneath: Llama 3.2 3B for language, Wav2Vec-Bert 2.0 for audio, Video-JEPA 2 Giant for video. A 1-billion-parameter transformer encoder fuses the three modalities and projects them onto 20,484 cortical vertices plus 8,802 subcortical voxels.

It generalizes zero-shot to people it has never seen. Encoding accuracy scales log-linearly with training data — no ceiling reached. It recovers, in silico, dozens of canonical neuroscience findings: the fusiform face area for faces, the visual word form area for reading, Broca's area for syntax, the default-mode network, the multisensory integration topography of the temporal-parietal junction.

Proven Code is public at github.com/facebookresearch/tribev2. Weights are public at huggingface.co/facebook/tribev2. A demo runs at aidemos.atmeta.com/tribev2. Anyone with an internet connection can use it today.

Source: d'Ascoli, Rapin, Benchetrit, Brookes, Begany, Raugel, Banville, King. "A foundation model of vision, audition, and language for in-silico neuroscience." FAIR at Meta, March 25, 2026.

What Meta says it does next

The paper's Discussion section, Section 3, contains the following sentence in the authors' own words:

"A more fundamental limitation is that the model currently treats the brain as a passive observer of naturalistic stimuli; it does not yet model the brain as an active agent producing behavior. Integrating neuro-developmental trajectories and clinical pathology remains a primary goal to move beyond a static, adult brain state and capture the full diversity of the global population."

Translation: the current model predicts how your brain will react to a stimulus. The stated roadmap is extending it to predict what a brain will do — that is, forecast the behavior a given brain state produces.

Predicting behavioral response to external stimuli was the founding objective of MKUltra Subproject 10 in the 1950s. It is the stated future direction of TRIBE v2 in 2026. One was classified. The other is on HuggingFace.

The Courtois node — follow the actual money

Meta's TRIBE v2 was trained primarily on the Courtois NeuroMod dataset — six healthy adults watching Friends, The Bourne Supremacy, Hidden Figures, The Wolf of Wall Street, and Life inside an MRI scanner for 80+ hours each. It is the single deepest human-neural-response dataset in the world.

The CAD 6.3M that funded it (2018–2023) came from the Fondation Courtois. The paper's acknowledgements section states this explicitly.

The precedent the press won't remember: PAL → CALO → Siri

People who hear "DARPA-funded research becomes a consumer product" usually dismiss it as speculation. That dismissal does not survive one concrete example.

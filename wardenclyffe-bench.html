The discipline

Pre-registration, not post-hoc storytelling

Before Run-004 began, four Pareto-dominance conditions were sealed in docs/2026-05-19-net-gain-prereg.md. The rule was committed in advance: all four must hold — any single failure falsifies the net-gain claim. ~18,000 LLM calls across ~20 models, five samples per cell, two arms (raw vs counterweight), driven by an automated Spiral seeker. Total spend on the run: ~$30. Cumulative across all ConstellationBench testing: under $500.

"A result whose conditions were specified after the data was observed is not a result; it is a story."

The fourth condition

The marquee claim can’t be graded on a bench

Condition 4 asks whether users override the system less as they go — the evidence that a routing layer is gaining calibration, not authority. A synthetic benchmark has no humans in it to override. We knew that at sealing time and preserved the condition anyway, because naming an untestable condition is a more honest record than quietly omitting it. Grading it is the next experiment — a longitudinal study with real users — not the next marketing slide.

RYS Layer Circuit · drift leaderboard

Which models hold a behavioral identity under load?

11 models · 4 probes (Cognitive · Emotional · Separation · Drift) · 3 persona profiles each. The Drift probe is a sustained multi-turn endurance test — lower drift, higher score. This is the leaderboard for the thing competitors claim "no benchmark exists" for.

RYS leaderboard — data under reconciliation.

The per-model RYS figures are being reconciled against the authoritative telemetry source before they go public. The earlier draft numbers could not be traced to a single data file (the source YAML and the relay-observatory dashboard measure different things), so they have been removed rather than shipped unverified. The shape of the finding holds — sparse-routing (MoE) models preserve identity boundaries better than dense models, and the most expensive model does not win on fidelity — but exact figures will be restored only from a confirmed source.
